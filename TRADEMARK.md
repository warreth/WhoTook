The **WhoTook** name and the WhoTook viewfinder logo are trademarks of the

statement that the mark is registered.

## The code and the name are separate

The source code is licensed under the **GNU AGPLv3**. That license gives you the
right to copy, modify, and redistribute the code. It does not give you the right
to use the WhoTook name or logo as the identity of your own build. This policy
does not reduce any right the AGPLv3 gives you over the code.

## Forks are welcome

You may fork WhoTook, change it, and distribute your version. If you distribute
a modified build, give it a different name and a different icon, and make clear
that it is not the official WhoTook app.

Do not use the WhoTook name or logo:

- as the name or icon of a fork, app, package, store listing, domain, or product;
- in a way that suggests your build is official, endorsed, or supported by the
  WhoTook project;
- for a paid product, service, or hosted version without permission.

## Uses that are fine

- Referring to WhoTook by name in a review, article, tutorial, or comparison.
- Saying that your project is "a fork of WhoTook" or "based on WhoTook", with a
  link to the official repository.
- Sharing unmodified official screenshots or builds, with a link to
  <https://whotook.party>.
- Using the name in academic or security research about the project.

## Permission

For any use of the name or logo that this policy does not allow, open an issue at
<https://github.com/warreth/WhoTook/issues> and describe what you want to do.
Permission is given in writing only.

## Formal registration

The project owner may register the mark in one or more jurisdictions. Until a
registration is granted, `™` is the correct symbol for the claimed unregistered
mark. Registration is a separate legal step and is not created by this document.
